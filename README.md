# Simulation-and-Energy-Integration-of-Crude-and-Vacuum-Distillation-Units-using-Aspen-Plus
1. This project focuses on the simulation and energy integration of a Crude Distillation Unit (CDU) and Vacuum Distillation Unit (VDU) using Aspen Plus V14. A refinery flowsheet was developed using the PetroFrac model and Peng–Robinson property method to simulate the fractionation of a blended crude feed into products such as naphtha, kerosene, diesel, and heavy vacuum gas oil.
2. The study includes crude characterization using assay data and pseudo-components, simulation of the crude preheat train, atmospheric distillation column, debutanizer, and vacuum column, followed by sensitivity analysis of key operating parameters such as furnace temperature, pump-around flow, steam rate, and feed rate.
3. Finally, pinch analysis was performed to evaluate heat recovery opportunities and determine minimum heating and cooling utility requirements, with the aim of improving the overall energy efficiency of the refinery process.
